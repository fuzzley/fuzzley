PATH, the Personal App for Technology Help, is a prototype of a mobile app made
for a Human Computer Interaction class. Each product page links to a simplified,
step-by-step manual alongside the original manual and videos.

It's a single-page app routed with hasher and crossroads.js, styled with
Bootstrap, with Knockout paging through the steps of the simplified manuals.
It's designed for phones and tablets, so it reads best on a small screen.
