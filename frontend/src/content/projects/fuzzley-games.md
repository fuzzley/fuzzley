A gallery of card and puzzle games to play in the browser, grouped into
Solitaire and Classics and searchable by name. The
[Solitaire Collection](/projects/solitaire/) and
[Minesweeper](/projects/minesweeper/) moved there from this site, and Sudoku
and Reversi are on the way.

The site is an Angular 22 single-page app built with Vite and styled with Sass.
The games are served beside it as static pages, from nginx on Google Cloud Run.
